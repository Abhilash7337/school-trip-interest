Take-home design exploration

Five directions for the School Trip Interest app

Each concept is a working prototype of the same brief: three trips, a teacher interest form with live validation, and an admin view with a trip filter and student totals. Submit the form, then switch to Admin to see your entry at the top of the table. Use Phone to check the mobile layout.

How they compare for a 48-hour take-home

My recommendation

Build Campus, and borrow the boarding-pass confirmation from Departures

Campus covers every line of the brief with the least code. It looks trustworthy to schools, works on phones without extra effort, and every part maps to plain Tailwind classes you can explain on the call.

Add one signature moment: when a teacher submits, show the boarding-pass confirmation with a reference code. It takes about an hour and gives the app personality without adding risk.

  • Form above the fold. Teachers can register without scrolling, which is the page's one job.
  • Admin is a real dashboard. KPIs, a filter that shows each trip's count and students, search and sort. This goes beyond "filter by trip, total per trip" without much extra code.
  • Dark mode comes almost free with CSS variables, a nice talking point.
  • Skip Orbit for this task. It's the most striking, but the canvas, glass and animation take about a day of polish and add code the brief didn't ask for.
